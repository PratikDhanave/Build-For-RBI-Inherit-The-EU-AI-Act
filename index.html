GAP 01

Mandatory AI kill switch

We have circuit breakers, deadlines, budget caps and deterministic fallbacks.

GAP 02

Ten-year retention

The inventory reads the live registry, so it cannot drift from what is running. The draft wants decommissioned models kept a decade.

A live inventory cannot drift, and cannot remember.

One thing that costs your customer, not you

Move a customer from deployer to provider
without telling them.

A company using AI under its own authority is a deployer. One that puts a system into service under its own name, or substantially modifies one, becomes a provider — carrying a far heavier set of legal duties.

Still a deployer

You install your agent, configured, under your name. Your customer operates it.

The heavy provider duties stay largely with you.

Duties stay with vendor

Now a provider

You fine-tune the model on their data, or ship it under their brand.

They have substantially modified a system — and the heavier duties are now theirs.

Heavier duties shift to client

That difference belongs in the SOW. Scoping conversation, not post-mortem.
