# Analogy Map — How Every Real-Life Picture Matches the Real System

**The one rule:** your *voice* speaks the left column. The *screen* shows the right column.
The moment you say "SHA-256" instead of "seal", the picture stops working.

Bodhyanga is the customer in the story. Every picture below is about keeping one promise to her.

## Pocket Card — eight anchor words, eight punchlines

| # | Anchor | One breath | Punchline |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | **Tag** | Data wears a bag tag; the wrong tag never boards. | "The wrong tag never flies." |
| 2 | **Pen** | We never erase her Yes — we write her Stop under it. | "New line. Never an eraser." |
| 3 | **Door** | One security door at the entrance, not a guard in every plane. | "62 planes, one door." |
| 4 | **License** | Passed the test as a scooter; driving a truck today. | "Valid license. Wrong vehicle." |
| 5 | **Seal** | Every page copies the seal of the page before it. | "Edit yesterday — today tears." |
| 6 | **Black box** | One safety core, two inspectors (India and Europe). | "Build once. Inspected twice." |
| 7 | **Plug** | A fuse trips itself; a plug needs a human hand. | "A fuse is an accident. A plug is a decision." |
| 8 | **Logo** | Rebrand or retrain the vendor's AI and its duties become yours. | "Your logo, your liability." |

Answer questions inside the same words: "that's a license question", "that's a seal question".

**Two upgrades for a banking room:** for #2 you may say **passbook** instead of notebook — "a passbook never erases a line; corrections are new lines." For #7, the **suspended bowler** explains the open gap best — "the umpire removes one bowler, and the match goes on." Every concept has a spare picture in the **Second Pictures** section at the end — switch pictures when a face looks lost; never repeat the same one louder.

## Second Pictures — a spare picture for every concept

The slides keep their pictures. These are *spoken* spares: use one when a face in the room looks lost, or when a question comes — a fresh picture beats repeating the old one louder.

**1 · Data stays in the country** *(slide picture: bag tag)*
- **OTT region lock:** "Her data follows the same rule as Netflix shows — not available outside India."
- Parcel pin code: wrong pin code, returned to sender, never delivered.

**2 · Consent that cannot be erased** *(slide picture: notebook in pen)*
- ⭐ **Bank passbook:** "A passbook never erases a line. A wrong entry gets a correcting line below it. Her Stop is a new line under her Yes."
- WhatsApp: even "delete for everyone" leaves "this message was deleted" — a trace always remains.

**3 · One gate for everything** *(slide picture: airport security)*
- **Society watchman:** "One watchman at the society gate — not one outside every flat."
- Cinema hall: one ticket-tear at the entrance, not inside every row.
- Note: the deck uses the airport for two different ideas (airline-vs-airport, and security). If the room mixes them up, switch this one to the watchman.

**4 · Approved for less, running as more** *(slide picture: scooter vs truck)*
- **Tourist visa, working job:** "Visa still valid. Wrong purpose."
- Domestic gas cylinder in a restaurant kitchen: the cylinder is fine; the use is illegal.

**5 · Records that cannot be quietly edited** *(slide picture: sealed diary)*
- **Numbered receipt book:** tear out one receipt and the stub and serial numbers betray you.
- Dahi-handi pyramid: pull one person from the middle layer and everyone above falls.

**6 · One build, two rulebooks** *(slide picture: black box)*
- ⭐ **Passport:** "One passport, stamped at the Indian counter and the German counter. Nobody carries two."

**7 · Switching one AI off, safely** *(slide picture: fuse vs plug)*
- ⭐ **Suspended bowler:** "The umpire removes one bowler — and the match goes on." (That "match goes on" part is exactly the hard engineering problem.)
- **Fan regulator → switch → MCB:** the three-step ladder in every Indian home — slow it, switch it off, trip the breaker — without darkening the house.

**8 · The user who became the maker** *(slide picture: tripwire)*
- **Relabeled water bottle:** "Pour it into bottles with your own sticker, and food-safety law now points at you. Your label on the bottle, your name in the court case."
- Franchise: run it as Domino's, their rulebook; rename it Pratik's Pizza — your kitchen, your lawsuits.
