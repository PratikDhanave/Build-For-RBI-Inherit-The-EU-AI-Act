Safety rules

  • Lost? Say the slide's anchor word. Breathe once. Click.
  • Notes are under every slide — glance, don't memorize.
  • Narrate the animation: say what the screen is doing.
  • They see it for the first time. Nothing you do is a mistake.
  • Demos: press R. Pre-warmed before doors.
  • If a demo stalls: go run labs/audit/main.go --test
  • After a demo lands: stop talking. Two full beats.
  • Slide 16: read it, 2-sec silence, repeat the ending slowly.
